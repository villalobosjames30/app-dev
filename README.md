# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: Inception

## 🧠 Overview
*Inception* is a science fiction film directed by **Christopher Nolan**.  
It explores the concept of **dreams within dreams** and challenges the boundaries between reality and imagination.

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Dom Cobb** – A skilled thief who steals secrets from within the subconscious.  
- **Arthur** – Cobb’s partner and point man.  
- **Ariadne** – The architect who designs dream worlds.  

## 💭 Why I Like It
> I love how the movie combines action, emotion, and philosophy.  
> The ending makes you question what’s real — it keeps you thinking long after it’s over.

## 🎵 Favorite Quote
> *“You mustn’t be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling.”* – Eames
